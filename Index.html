ABOUT MPOWER

Every Meal Saved
Creates Another Smile.

MPower is a community-driven platform dedicated to reducing food waste by connecting restaurants, supermarkets and households with orphanages, charities and food banks.

Together we ensure that surplus food reaches people instead of landfills, creating a healthier, more compassionate and sustainable future.

HOW IT WORKS

Four Simple Steps To Change Lives

MPower makes donating food simple, fast and impactful. Every donation follows a safe and transparent process.

Donate

Restaurants, supermarkets and households list surplus food.

Match

Nearby charities receive instant notifications.

Deliver

Volunteers collect and transport the food safely.

Empower

Families and communities receive healthy meals.
